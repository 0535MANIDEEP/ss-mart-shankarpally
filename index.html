The same shop, more to walk into

Shankarpally has known Sai Sangameshwara Kirana and General for years. The counter, the phone number and the people behind it have not changed. What has changed is the inside: it is a supermarket now, properly lit and properly laid out, with the whole weekly shop in one place instead of a trip to two or three.

Nothing about the way it is run has changed. It is still a shop you walk into, still the same people, and still the one place on BNR Road for the household shop.
